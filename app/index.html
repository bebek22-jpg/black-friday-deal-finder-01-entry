How every price was checked

Each deal on this board was found in a store's own Black Friday sale or a deals roundup that names the store, and its sale price and usual price were read from that source on Friday 27 November 2026 (Black Friday itself). The check time and the source sit on every card. A deal whose price could not be confirmed was left off the board.

Real deal? REAL DEAL means a true low or a rare deep cut. STRONG DEAL is a big, honest saving. SOLID DEAL is worth it but not historic. ROUTINE SALE is a small cut — the Nintendo Switch 2 bundle, for example, is only 9% off, but it is the first $50 cut on a brand-new console, so it stays on the board with the honest label.

Discounts are computed from the two prices shown, so they always add up. Prices can move or sell out — the store link on each card is the place to confirm before you buy.
