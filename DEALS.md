## Notes on judgement calls

- LG OLED C6: Amazon lists $2,700, but CNN notes it "has more
  accurately been selling for $1,800 over the past few months" — the
  board uses $1,800 as the honest usual price, not the inflated list.
- Levi's jeans: CNN reports 55% off at $50; the $111 usual price is
  the arithmetic usual (50 / 0.45), and the board's 55% matches it.
- Hisense U6 Pro: CNN reports a 52% discount at $530; the $1,100
  usual price is the arithmetic usual (530 / 0.48).
- MacBook Air M5: Amazon lists the same spec at $989 ($310 off);
  the board shows Best Buy's $1,049 and notes the Amazon price.
- Switch 2 bundle: only 9% off, kept on the board with a ROUTINE
  label because it is the first $50 cut on a brand-new console —
  the honest signal matters more than padding the average.
